# Privacy

PortableGrid runs on your computer inside Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop. It has no account, no analytics and
no tracking.

- Your designs, presets and exported files stay on your computer.
- Once a day it asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer version exists, so it can tell you about
  updates. The request contains no personal data. Turn it off in the panel's settings.
- Adobe Exchange and GitHub, where you download PortableGrid, have their own privacy policies.

Questions: https://github.com/vicenet/PortableGrid/issues
