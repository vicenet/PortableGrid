# Adobe Exchange listing: PortableGrid

Everything needed to publish PortableGrid on Adobe Exchange, where Illustrator and Photoshop users look
for plugins.

## Listing text

**Name:** PortableGrid

**Short description (one line):**
Free layout grids for Illustrator and Photoshop: columns, baselines, golden ratio, magazine layouts and creator safe zones.

**Description:**
PortableGrid builds clean, precise grids in one click and helps you design on them.

- Column, modular, baseline and hierarchical grids with any unit in any field, and a live preview with draggable margins.
- Composition guides: rule of thirds, phi grid, golden spiral, golden triangles and harmonic armature.
- Drawing grids: square (8pt), dot, isometric, triangle and hexagon.
- Harmonious grids from one number: enter your body text size and get the baseline, margins, gutters and rows, all locked together.
- Magazine page layouts in Illustrator: image frames, threaded text on the baseline grid, captions, pull quotes, running heads and page numbers, styled with editable paragraph styles. Use your own text, images or a PortableStudio brand.
- Snap to grid and Grid check: move objects onto the grid in one click, and score how clean a layout is.
- Creator safe zones for Instagram Story and Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, thumbnails and channel banners.
- Arrange images into cells, split artwork into tiles and export them as numbered images for Instagram grids and carousels.
- Grids are saved in the document, follow artboard resizes, and every action is a single undo.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). No account, no subscription.

**Keywords:** grid, layout grid, guides, baseline grid, column grid, modular grid, golden ratio, rule of thirds, magazine layout, editorial design, safe zone, instagram, isometric grid, typography, design system

## How to submit

1. Sign in at **developer.adobe.com** with your Adobe ID and open **Distribute** (Adobe Developer Distribution).
2. Create a new listing for a **CEP extension**, and upload the signed `PortableGrid-<version>.zxp` from the
   latest release (built by `npm run package`).
3. Paste the text below, upload the icon and screenshots from `.github/exchange/`, and choose the hosts
   (Illustrator and Photoshop, 2021 and later).
4. Choose **Free**, add the support and privacy links, and submit for review. Adobe reviews listings
   before they go live, which can take a few days to a few weeks.

Adobe's exact requirements (image sizes, text limits) change from time to time: the portal shows them
next to each field. Resize the images in `.github/exchange/` if it asks for different sizes.
